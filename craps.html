Craps

Premise

This is a classic dice game where you bet on whether or not the player will re-roll their first roll before rolling seven. Having seven or eleven as an initial roll lets the rolling player win while an initial two, three, or twelve leads to their loss.

Strategy

There's no strategy - but the bets greatly vary in their house advantages.

Odds on (don't) pass/come will reduce the house edge but increase volatility.

You should Place 6/8 and 5/9 instead of Buying 6/8 or 5/9 or Big 6/8 - in contrast Buying 4/10 is better than Placing 4/10

Ultimate Texas Hold'Em

Premise

This game involves three rounds of betting on whether your 5-card poker hand is better than the dealers using two hole cards and five community cards.

Strategy

First Bet

Raise ×3 with an Ten or better, otherwise, check

Second Bet

Raise ×2 with pair, flush, or straight - or if you have a 9 and the two community cards are over Seven.

Otherwise, check

Third Bet

Bet ×1 if you have a Five or better.

Otherwise, fold

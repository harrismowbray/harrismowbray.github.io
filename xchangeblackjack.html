XChange Blackjack

Premise

This game is based on blackjack - but you're allowed to double your initial wager to replace your second card, provided your cards are the same color. All non-blackjack hands push if the Dealer gets 22.

House Edge

Main Bet

Blackjack 3 to 2

House Edge: 1.04% | RTP: 98.96%

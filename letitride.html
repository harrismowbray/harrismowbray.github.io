Let It Ride

Premise

This game involves three rounds of betting on whether you can make a good 5-card poker hand with your two cards and three community cards that are sequentially revealed.

Strategy

First Bet

If you have a paying hand (tens or better, flush, straight, etc.), or three to a straight flush, let it ride.

Otherwise, take your first bet.

Second Bet

If you have a paying hand, or four to a flush or straight, let it ride.

Otherwise, take your second bet.

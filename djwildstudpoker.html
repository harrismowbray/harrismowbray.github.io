DJ Wild Stud Poker

Premise

This game is based on poker - but you're only dealt five cards and bet you have a better hand than a dealer. All 2's are wild, and there is a joker in the deck too!

Strategy

Raise if you have a pair of fives or better (straight, flush, etc.)

Also raise if you have a pair of Fours, as long as you don't also have a Three.

Otherwise, fold

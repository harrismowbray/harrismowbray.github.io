Roulette

Premise

You guess where a ball will land on a wheel - all bets essentially have the same house edge.

House Edge

Main Bets

French Roulette

House Edge: 1.35% | RTP: 98.65%

Main Bets

Single Zero (0)

House Edge: 2.70% | RTP: 97.30%

Main Bets

Double Zero (00)

House Edge: 5.26% | RTP: 94.74%

Main Bets

Triple Zero (000)

House Edge: 7.69% | RTP: 92.31%

Main Bets

Quadruple Zero (0000)

House Edge: 10.00% | RTP: 90.00%

Strategy

No strategy, except you should avoid the higher house edge of the 00-0-1-2-3 bet in double or quadruple zero roulette. Additionally, play even-money bets if 'la partage' is available (French roulette) for a lower house edge.

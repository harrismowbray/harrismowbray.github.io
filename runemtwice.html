Run 'Em Twice

Premise

This game is based on poker - but you're only dealt two cards which you combine with a cross-shaped layout of five cards to try to form the best hand across and down - making sequential wagers wagers as the cards are revealed.

Strategy

First Bet

Raise ×3 with a pair or suited Jack/Queen

Fold if your cards are not a pair, unsuited, and both 5 or less

Otherwise, bet ×1

Second Bet

Same strategy as the first bet

Third Bet

Bet ×3 with a pair of sixes or better in combination with either the vertical or horizontal cards.

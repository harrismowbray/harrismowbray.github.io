Crazy 4 Poker

Premise

In this game, the dealer and player both receive five cards, and the player wants to make a better 4-card poker hand than the dealer.

House Edge

Main Bet

Four Aces 200 to 1

Four of a Kind 30 to 1

Straight Flush 15 to 1

Three of a Kind 2 to 1

Flush 3 to 2

Straight 1 to 1

House Edge: 3.42% | RTP: 96.58%

Straight Flush 30 to 1

Three of a Kind 9 to 1

Flush 4 to 1

Straight 3 to 1

Two Pair 2 to 1

Queens or Better 1 to 1

House Edge: 3.06% | RTP: 96.94%

Queens Up

Four of a Kind 50 to 1

Three of a Kind 8 to 1

Flush 4 to 1

Straight 3 to 1

Two Pair 2 to 1

Queens or Better 1 to 1

House Edge: 4.52% | RTP: 95.48%

Strategy

Play if your hand is a King-Queens-Eight-Six or better, otherwise fold

Play King-Queen-Seven-Six-(Five) if there are three or four suits in your hand

Play King-Queen-Seven-Six-(Four) if all four suits are present in your hand

Face-Up Pai Gow Poker

Premise

This game is based on poker - you're dealt two cards and sequentially bet as three community cards are revealed - with the goal of creating the best 5-card poker hand.

House Edge

Main Bet

Player Win 1 to 1

Dealer Ace-High Push

Strategy

There is not really any strategy - there are 21 ways to arrange your hand, just choose one that lets you win, or at least push. The casino's House Way will not always give you the best way to arrange your hand.

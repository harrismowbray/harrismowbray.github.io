Four Card Poker

Premise

This game involves betting on whether your 4-card poker hand is better than the dealer's

House Edge

Main Bet

Four of a Kind 25 to 1

Straight Flush 20 to 1

Three of a Kind 2 to 1

House Edge: 2.79% | RTP: 97.21%

Aces Up

Four of a Kind 50 to 1

Straight Flush 30 to 1

Three of a Kind 7 to 1

Flush 6 to 1

Straight 5 to 1

Two Pair 2 to 1

Pair of Aces 1 to 1

House Edge: 3.37% | RTP: 96.63%

Aces Up

Four of a Kind 50 to 1

Straight Flush 40 to 1

Three of a Kind 8 to 1

Flush 6 to 1

Straight 4 to 1

Two Pair 2 to 1

Pair of Aces 1 to 1

House Edge: 4.24% | RTP: 95.76%

Strategy

Bet ×3 with a pair of Aces or better (straight, flush, etc.)

With a pair of Kings, bet ×3 except ×1 if the dealer is showing an Ace and you lack an Ace or a 4.

With a pair of Jacks or Queens, bet ×3 except ×1 if the dealer's card is higher than your pair and doesn't match a card in your hand.

With a pair of Tens or Nines, bet ×3 except ×1 if the dealer's card is higher than your pair.

With a pair of Eights, bet ×3 except ×1 if the dealer's card is a Two.

With a pair of Fours, Fives, Sixes and Sevens: bet ×1.

With a pair of Threes, bet ×1 unless the dealer has a Jack and you don't have a Jack or higher.

With a pair of Twos or AKQ, bet ×1 if the dealer's card matches a card in your hand.

AKJT, bet ×1 if the dealer's card is a Jack

AQJT-(9), bet x1 if the dealer's card is a Nine

Otherwise, fold

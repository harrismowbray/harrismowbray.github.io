Hi-Lo Mojo

Premise

This is a game where you keep guessing if the next card will be higher or lower, each round you can cash out or keep playing. The dealer drawing an 8 leads to a push.

Strategy

Round 1

If the first card is 7 or 9, cash out

Round 2

Don't cash out

Round 3

Cash out if the last card was a 6, 7, 9, or 10

Round 4-8

Cash out if the last card was a 7 or 9

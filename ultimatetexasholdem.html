Premise

Strategy

First Bet

Raise ×4 with an Ace, a King with a 5 or better, a Queen with an 8 or better, or a Jack/10

Additionally, if your cards are suited, raise ×4 with a King / 2-4, a Queen / 6-7, or a Jack / 8-9

If you have a pair (except 2's), raise ×4

Otherwise, check

Second Bet

Raise ×2 with a two pair or better, if you have four to a flush, or a pair involving exactly one of your hole cards

Otherwise, check

Third Bet

Bet ×1 if you have a pair, straight, or flush involving one or two of your hole cards

If the dealer has 20 or less remaining cards that could beat you, bet ×1

Otherwise, fold

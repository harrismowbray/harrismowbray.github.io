Mississippi Stud

Premise

This game is based on poker - you're dealt two cards and sequentially bet as three community cards are revealed - with the goal of creating the best 5-card poker hand.

Strategy

First Bet

Raise ×3 with a pair

Raise ×1 if you have a Jack or better, or if neither card is 5 or below

Finally, raise ×1 with suited 6/5

Otherwise, fold

Second Bet

Raise, ×3 with a pair of sixes or better

Raise, ×3 if you have the cards needed for a straight flush

Raise, ×1 if you have the cards needed for a straight or flush

Finally, raise ×1 if you have a pair of 2s-5s

Otherwise, fold

Third Bet

Same strategy as the second bet

If you bet ×3 earlier, don't fold - bet ×1

Otherwise, fold

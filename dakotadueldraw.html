Dakota Duel Draw

Premise

This game is based on poker - but you're only dealt two cards which you combine with a two sets of three community cards to try to form the best 5-card poker hand possible.

House Edge

Main Bet

Straight Flush 50 to 1

Flush 6 to 1
Straight 4 to 1

Nines or Better 2 to 1

House Edge: 3.02% | RTP: 96.98%

Strategy

Raise if you have a card higher than 8, if your cards are suited (except 2/7, 2/8, and 3/8), or form a pair

Otherwise, fold
